How CampusFind Works

Recovering misplaced possessions across college grounds takes three structured steps.

1

Report Lost or Found

Post item details, approximate campus location, date, color, and upload clear reference photographs.

2

Rule-Based Smart Match

Our weighted criteria engine automatically cross-references categories, locations, dates, and keywords.

3

QR Verified Handover

CampusFind is a dedicated lost and found ecosystem designed to streamline campus property recovery. Built with high performance, enterprise reliability, and privacy at its foundation, it empowers students, faculty, and security personnel to collaborate seamlessly.
